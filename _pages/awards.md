## Doctoral Grants & Fellowships

### **CIFRE Doctoral Grant** *(2024 – 2027)*
- **Partners**: [Ejara](https://ejara.io), [Université Paris-Est Créteil (UPEC)](https://www.u-pec.fr), and [ANRT (Association Nationale de la Recherche et de la Technologie)](https://www.anrt.asso.fr)
- **Topic**: *An Opportunistic Mesh Network for P2P Cryptocurrency Transactions based on a Resilient Blockchain Infrastructure*
- Fully funded CIFRE fellowship supporting doctoral research bridging academic rigor with real-world financial infrastructure for internet-constrained regions.
